Example scope

Keep a small service genuinely small.

The point of this page is to show how the concept separates straightforward customer-facing fixes from work that would need a bigger project or a specialist.

The basic rule

Start with public information. Agree a narrow change before access is needed. If the work is risky or too large, stop and hand it to the right person.

Example deliverables

What a small handover could include

  • Short notes on the customer-facing problem.
  • Before and after screenshots for any agreed change.
  • A plain-English summary of what changed.
  • Anything that could not be changed safely.
  • One sensible next step rather than a long sales list.

Safe access

The first check in the concept uses public links. If an agreed change needed account access, I would design the process around platform roles such as collaborator, editor or manager rather than asking somebody to send a password.

Outside the example scope

Full rebuilds, SEO campaigns, paid ads, e-commerce, payment systems, complex booking systems, hacked-site recovery, branding and social-media management are deliberately outside this small-service idea.
